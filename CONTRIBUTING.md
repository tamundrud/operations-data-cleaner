# Contributing

Operations Data Cleaner is an internal release candidate under qualification. Changes follow the approved implementation plan; publication and final release acceptance require separate approval.

Before proposing substantial changes, read the [PRD](docs/PRD.md) and [agent instructions](AGENTS.md). Open an issue describing the operational problem, user impact, and proposed approach. Avoid scope expansion beyond the approved MVP plan.

For pull requests, keep changes focused, describe the relevant issue or plan, include automated checks where applicable, explain data-fidelity or privacy risks, and document any limitations.

**Never post real operational, customer, employee, employer, invoice, or personally identifying data.** All test fixtures and examples must be synthetic.

By contributing, you agree your contribution can be distributed under the project's Apache-2.0 license. You must have the necessary rights to contribute your code or data. No separate contributor-license agreement is currently required. A future change to relicensing policies would require its own review.
